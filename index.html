        Record Installed Serials

        Type the serial numbers already fitted to each aircraft. Each one is saved as a first fit hardware record, so it appears on the Hardware tab's fitment list and on the aircraft's Installed Equipment panel. Anything already on record is shown and left alone — log a replacement from Activity → Add New Activity.

        Press Enter to jump to the next serial field. Entries are kept while you switch between the two views.

        Record Removed Serials

        Units that came off and were never recorded. Add as many as you need per aircraft — each becomes a removed unit in the register. Dates and reasons are optional: capture the serials now and complete them later on the Serials tab.
